# Pin 53 — pricing m=137 against the Tier-2 ceiling (2026-07-27)

**PRICED, NOT CHOSEN.** Owner pin 53
(`docs/superpowers/2026-07-27-owner-ruling-crn-sigma-rule0.md`, PART 6):

> DO NOT DECIDE THE REMEDY NOW. T14 collapses F_ens by design — the very quantity
> the m question turns on. The decision belongs after T14/T15 with Rule 0.b, where
> it already sits. What is needed before then: price m=137 against the Tier-2
> ceiling that already holds T5 at m=100, since RAM scales with m and the option
> may be blocked in practice however cheap it looks in principle. Price it; don't
> choose.

Where 137 comes from: pin 43's settling measurement puts the σ instrument's CLEAN
cell out of reach at m=100 for every factor ≥ 1.00, with `min m for CLEAN` = 129 at
factor 1.00, **137 at 1.03**, 148 at 1.07. See
`docs/superpowers/2026-07-27-phase14-pin43-settling-measurement.md` §4.

## Headline

**m=137 is not the binding constraint on either axis. It neither unblocks T5 nor
newly blocks it.** RAM moves by **+0.5%** on the model and the wall bound moves by
**×1.37** — smaller than the box's own measured throughput drift of ×1.70 (pin 28).
T5 remains blocked for the reasons it was already blocked.

## 1. RAM

### 1.1 What the sizing model says

`size_tile` on the recorded T5 diverse-tile configuration (19° geometry,
`n_grid_nodes=9216`, `n_windows=9`, `n_obs=35802`, `window_days=60`; `nnz`
reproduces the recorded 127,110,299 exactly):

| m | `peak_model_mib` | `retained_member_store_mib` | Tier-1 predicate needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 4715.6 | 63.3 | 9431 MiB |
| 129 | 4733.9 | 81.6 | 9468 MiB |
| **137** | **4739.0** | **86.7** | **9478 MiB** |
| 148 | 4745.9 | 93.7 | 9492 MiB |

m=100 → m=137 costs **+23.4 MiB of predicted peak (+0.5%)** and **+47 MiB on the
`tier1_eligible` threshold**, against a predicate that at T5 pricing time failed by
~4,170 MiB (`MemAvailable` 5261 vs 9431 required). **The m change is noise against
that gap.**

### 1.2 Why it is so small — and where the knee actually is

`peak_model` returns a phase-MAX. Its m-linear terms are `rhs_batch`,
`solve_workspace` and `retained`, costing **9.0893 MiB per member** on this
geometry. The m-free assembly phase (`csr_g` + `assembly_triplets` + baseline) is
**4650.1 MiB**. The phase-max therefore does not track m at all until the solve
phase overtakes assembly:

    4650.1 / 9.0893 = 511.6  ->  parity at m ~ 512

| m | total | m-linear | m-free |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 4715.6 | 908.9 | 4650.1 |
| 137 | 4739.0 | 1245.2 | 4650.1 |
| 500 | 6287.6 | 4544.6 | 4650.1 |
| **512** | **6396.7** | **4653.7** | **4650.1** |
| 600 | 7196.5 | 5453.6 | 4650.1 |

**This closes a loop worth recording.** Pin 53 says the original m-rejection rested
on **~512 members**. That is exactly the RAM knee: 512 is where raising m starts
costing real memory. So the earlier rejection was not arbitrary — it was priced at
the knee. **m=137 sits at 27% of the knee, deep inside the flat region.**

### 1.3 ⛔ The model's known defect — stated, not hidden

The T5 price bracket (§1.7,
`docs/superpowers/2026-07-26-phase14-stage1-t5-price-bracket.md`) already recorded
that **the model's peak is m-insensitive by construction** over this range, and
called that a defect. It is the same defect here, so **§1.1 must not be used alone
to clear m=137.** Measured-vs-model ratios do not even agree in direction:

| run | measured `peak_rss_mib` | model | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| anchor gate (m=100, 9 windows) | 3512.19 | 1650.8 (preflight) | **2.13** (under-predicts) |
| Stage-1 probe row | 3662.18 | 4652.34 | 0.787 (over-predicts) |
| seam tile (m=100, as run) | 2573.52 | 1404.82 | **1.83** (under-predicts) |

### 1.4 Empirical bracket instead

- **Lower bound (model):** +23 MiB. Known to understate m.
- **Upper bound (all measured peak scales linearly in m):** ×1.37. This is a
  *bound*, not an estimate — the assembly phase is genuinely m-free, so the truth
  sits near the lower end.

| configuration | measured peak at m=100 | ×1.37 upper bound |
|---|---|---|
| seam pair (as run) | 2573.5 MiB | 3525.7 MiB |
| anchor gate | 3512.2 MiB | 4811.7 MiB |
| T5 honest expected actual | 6.6 – 10.0 GiB | 9.0 – 13.7 GiB |

**The T5 upper bound (13.7 GiB) exceeds this box's total RAM (15 GiB) once the
co-tenant is present** — but so does much of the m=100 bracket's upper half, which
is why T5 is already owner-held. m=137 does not create that condition.

## 2. Wall

Member-batch legs scale ~linearly in m; the mean solve does not. Upper bound ×1.37:

| configuration | measured at m=100 | ×1.37 upper bound |
|---|---|---|
| seam pair (both tiles, full leg) | 55,201.65 s = 15.33 h | 21.0 h |
| anchor gate | 22,352.06 s = 6.21 h | 8.51 h |
| T5, per tile | 23.8 – 94.2 h | 32.6 – 129.1 h |
| T5, four diverse tiles | 95 – 377 h (4.0 – 15.7 d) | 130 – 516 h (5.4 – 21.5 d) |

**Against the only pre-registered Tier-2 ceiling** (`tier2_probe`,
`max_wall_h = 6.0`): T5's low end is over by **4.0×** at m=100 and by **5.4×** at
m=137. Over is over; the multiplier changes, the verdict does not.

**Put the ×1.37 in proportion:** pin 28 measured **×1.70 throughput drift inside a
single run** on this host (identical-geometry windows ranged 1,656–3,480 s;
`seam_s` ran ~30% slower than `seam_n` at identical geometry). **The m=100→137
factor is smaller than the box's own non-stationarity.** Any wall-based argument
for or against m=137 is inside that noise.

## 3. What this does NOT settle

1. **No remedy is chosen.** Pin 53 is explicit: T14 collapses `F_ens` by design, so
   the m decision belongs after T14/T15, with Rule 0.b, in T17.
2. **Scope is not settled either.** m=137 is the σ instrument's requirement; which
   RUNS would have to carry it is a separate question this pricing does not answer.
   Under the current board that would touch the seam pair and any T5 σ rows —
   **named as an open question, not resolved.**
3. **The m=137 figure is itself provisional.** It comes from factor 1.03, and no
   factor is adopted (129 at 1.00, 148 at 1.07). Pin 43(a)'s caveat — partitions
   share draws and understate the null spread — applies to the factor and therefore
   to the m figure derived from it.
4. **T14 may move the requirement entirely.** Pairing the CRN collapses `F_ens`,
   which is the denominator the whole m question turns on. The number priced here
   could be obsolete the moment T14 lands, which is precisely why pin 53 ordered it
   priced and not chosen.
